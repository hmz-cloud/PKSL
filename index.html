The Strategist

The evolution from Technology Evangelist to Independent Brand Strategist.

My foundation isn't just in design; it's rooted deeply in technology. Before 2017, I spent years working as a Technology Evangelist, collaborating closely with multiple IT partners to bring complex solutions to market.

Applying that technical insight to business creation, I founded multiple ventures between 2017 and 2018. Over time, that vision naturally evolved toward branding and digital marketing, culminating in the launch of PICKSIL in 2021.

After running PICKSIL as a standard, multi-disciplinary agency for several years, I recognized that clients achieve the best results through direct, undivided collaboration. Today, I operate entirely solo. You receive the strategic depth and technical foresight of an agency, delivered with the agility and personal dedication of an independent partner.
